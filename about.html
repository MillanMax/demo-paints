About Demo Paints

A fictional paint maker with a very real passion for colour that lasts.

Our story

Born in Kampala, built for the tropics

Demo Paints began in 1998 in a small Kampala workshop with one mixer and a simple idea: paint should survive the sun, the rain and the real world. Today we supply homeowners, contractors and industry across East Africa.

This site is a demonstration — the brand, products, prices and dealers are fictional.

Mission

Make every surface last longer with honest, high-performance coatings.

Vision

The most trusted paint brand in East Africa by 2035.

Our journey

1998

Workshop opens in Kampala's Industrial Area.

2006

First automated emulsion plant commissioned.

2013

Roof and industrial coatings range launched.

2019

Low-VOC water-based lines become our standard.

2025

Ten dealer locations and an online colour studio.

Quality & environment

Tested hard, made responsibly

Every batch passes viscosity, opacity, gloss and adhesion checks. Our water-based ranges are low-VOC, and we recycle process water and offer tin take-back at dealers.
